Double D Roofing
Operational Flows

1. Residential Roofing Job

1
Staff creates proposal
2
Customer receives link and signs digitally
3
Signed proposal converts to invoice
4
Customer pays via Stripe (card), Cash, or Check
5
Job is scheduled (Google Calendar optional)
6
Photos and notes added
7
Job closed

2. Commercial Job

1
Same as residential, with these additions:
•
Job type = Commercial
•
8.25% sales tax calculated and shown on proposal and invoice
•
Payment options: Stripe, Cash, or Check

3. Membership Enrollment — Double D Install

1
Roof installed by Double D with qualifying GAF product
2
Warranty type recorded
3
Staff enrolls customer in a tier (calculator used if size/pitch adjustment needed)
4
Measurement report upload (recommended, not required)
If measurements come from insurance papers or another source, store the numbers and note the source (e.g. “Insurance papers – 2024 claim”).
5
Customer signs membership agreement
6
Membership becomes active with visit schedule

4. Membership Enrollment — Existing Roof (Not Double D Install)

1
Initial inspection performed
2
Qualifying repairs completed if needed
3
Original install date and product type (if known) recorded
4
Inspection report / measurement upload (recommended, not required)
Same as above — can note source if measurements come from insurance papers or elsewhere.
5
Staff enrolls customer in appropriate tier
6
Customer signs membership agreement
7
Membership becomes active

5. Ongoing Membership Visits

1
Upcoming visits appear on staff dashboard
2
Team completes inspection / gutter cleaning
3
Notes and photos added
4
Visit marked complete
5
Next visit scheduled
6
Customer can see history and request post-storm inspection

6. Upload Insurance Papers

1
Staff or customer uploads insurance documents (PDF, photos of papers, adjuster reports, etc.)
2
Documents are stored permanently with the customer / property record
3
Staff adds a note describing the source and any key details (e.g. “2024 hail claim – squares and pitch taken from adjuster report”)
4
Measurement numbers (if present) can be entered into the membership calculator or property record
5
Documents remain available to staff and the customer in the portal
This flow can be used during membership enrollment, a new job, or at any time the customer provides insurance paperwork.

7. Customer Portal

•
View membership status, warranty, next visit
•
View and sign proposals
•
Pay invoices (Stripe, or see instructions for cash/check)
•
See photos, measurement reports, and insurance papers
•
Request post-storm inspection
•
Direct contact option (phone, email, or simple form)
•
Simple question-answering bot for common customer service questions (optional for beta)
